Privacy Policy

This policy covers the privately operated Hermes Agent installation described on this site, not other installations or the upstream open-source project.

Access and use

The application uses Google OAuth permissions to access Google account information and Workspace content needed for the owner's requests. Depending on granted permissions and requested tasks, this may include files, spreadsheets, documents, email, calendar entries and their metadata. Granting a permission does not mean that all data in that service is routinely collected.

Processing and service providers

Data relevant to a request may be processed on the operator's server and sent to configured AI or other service providers when needed to perform the request. Those providers process data according to their own terms and policies. This site does not promise that all processing remains on the operator's server. The operator does not sell Google user data.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's server to maintain authorized access. Task content may be retained in conversation history, logs, working files or backups. Retention depends on the installation's configuration and the owner's needs; this policy does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period.

Control and deletion

The account owner can revoke Google access at Google account permissions. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not itself remove previously stored files or conversation records. For deletion of retained data, contact the operator.
